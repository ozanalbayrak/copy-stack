CopyStack

A stack of ready-to-paste snippets, drawn as one front-facing object: two sheets stepping back behind a single card, with a snippet line and a ⌘ on its face.

01 — Three concepts

A — FAN

Sheets fanned up and to the right, the top one lifting off — carries motion and "many snippets", but the diagonal collapses into a smudge below 32 px.

B — STEPPED STACK Selected

Front-facing stack: two sheets step back behind one large card carrying a snippet line and a ⌘. Symmetrical, quiet, native — and the silhouette still reads at 16 px.

C — KEYCAP

A keycap pressing down onto two sheets. The keyboard idea leads, which makes it read as a shortcut or keyboard utility rather than a store of snippets.

02 — B, rendered

The object is four filled shapes, no strokes: two translucent sheets, the card, and its slightly cooler rim. Depth comes from the step between layers and the ground gradient behind them — no long shadow, no texture, no inner glow.

  • Indigo ground keeps it clear of Notes yellow, Reminders red, Finder and Mail blue.
  • Amber ⌘ is the only accent, and the only non-white mark that survives to 32 px.
  • Lightest stop sits at the top, so it sinks correctly next to Apple's icons in a Login Items row.
  • Everything is one centred silhouette: nothing crosses the squircle edge, nothing floats outside it.
